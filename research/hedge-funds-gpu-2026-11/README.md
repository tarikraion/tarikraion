# Hedge funds that could buy GPU capacity (4 Nov 2026)

Scope: hedge funds in the UK, Europe, the US, Singapore and the Middle East, with a dated 2025–2026 public signal that they need compute.

- "Hedge fund" means a firm that runs outside money in pooled funds. Prop trading firms and sovereign funds are listed separately below.
- Already covered in `gpu-icp-2026-10`, so excluded here: Man Group, XTX Markets, Hudson River Trading, Jane Street, Citadel Securities, EquiLibre.
- Tiers:
  - **A:** firms that already buy or run GPU and HPC capacity.
  - **B:** firms building AI or ML capability, with a compute need that is likely but not proven.
  - **C:** firms using AI mainly through hosted cloud models.
- The `verification` column says whether I re-checked the signal myself or it rests only on the research agent's sources.
- No LinkedIn profile URL appeared in any search result, so every one is "not found". None were guessed.

## Singapore and the Middle East

- **Hedge funds based there:** only one has a qualifying signal, Dymon Asia (Singapore, tier C). No hedge fund headquartered in the Middle East or Hong Kong qualified.
- **US funds building in the region:**
  - Citadel moved Hong Kong quant researchers to Singapore and Miami in May 2026 (FT via Bloomberg/Reuters).
  - Millennium is advertising for a new systematic equities quant pod in Dubai (job ads, Mar–Jul 2026).
- **Sovereign funds, not hedge funds:**
  - GIC (Singapore) is staffing a new "AI Alpha Group" (job ads, mid-2026).
  - ADIA (Abu Dhabi) has a quant R&D unit and launched a 2026 quant residency.
  - Mubadala said in Dec 2025 that it "needs more compute". It is a G42 shareholder, so it will likely use UAE capacity.
- **Mainland China (not a prospect):** Ubiquant (320B-parameter model) and High-Flyer (DeepSeek's parent) need large compute, but selling Blackwell capacity to them raises export-control problems.
