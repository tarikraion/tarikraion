## Langdock (Langdock SE) (Berlin, Germany), fit 7/10
- **Signal:** Co-CEO Judith Dada's essay on European compute demand: Langdock pools demand from many organisations too small to underwrite compute alone. 'In a sense, we are unlocking our own offtake and now building our own European data centres on the back of this.' (2026-11-24, https://dadalogue.substack.com/p/oh-tokens-where-art-thou)
- **Need:** 1 block. Estimate only; no GPU count is disclosed. The stated EUR 10-15M first-site budget is about one 0.5 MW block (200-250 Blackwell GPUs). The workload is managed open-model inference for ~13,000 organisations, and the company says it will 'start small and expand' with demand. A second block is plausible in 2027 if open-model traffic moves off Azure-hosted frontier APIs.
- **Urgency:** NOW / Q1-Q2 2027
- **Cash flow:** positive (company/investor-stated, unaudited)
- **Compute today:** Multi-tenant SaaS hosted on Microsoft Azure servers inside the EU (https://langdock.com/enterprise, per search summary). It routes to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Mistral, Meta and other models (Sacra). It plans its own German data centre(s) for open-source models. No neocloud deal is known. Risk: an own-build, 'European hyperscaler' strategy could make it a builder or competitor rather than a renter. Pitch a dedicated German/UK block as a faster, capex-free first or overflow site.
- **For and against:** For: founder-led, profitable, very fast growth ($1M to ~$50-67M ARR in about two years), sovereign/data-residency mandate (moved to an EU SE over the CLOUD Act), and a fresh public statement that it is building European compute now. Against: no round in 12 months and a tiny equity base ($3.5M), so a 12-36 month ~$7M/yr commitment rests on operating cash flow. It may prefer to own hardware (the stated plan is its 'own data centre').

## AlphaSense (New York, NY, US (large engineering hubs in Helsinki and London)), fit 5/10
- **Signal:** 'Agents now run 99% of the searches on AlphaSense. In October, we were processing tokens at an annualized rate of roughly 75 trillion, and that number keeps climbing.' Company-reported; Dealroom/Digg headlines put this at up 12-fold. (2026-11-12, https://www.alpha-sense.com/resources/technical-articles/superanalyst-cost-efficiency/)
- **Need:** 1 block. Estimate only (1-2 blocks). It needs RL post-training of a ~30B open-weight model on millions of trajectories, plus serving that custom context-collection model for part of a 75T-token/yr, still-growing workload. Most reasoning stays on frontier APIs, so the self-hosted share is uncertain.
- **Urgency:** Q1-Q2 2027 (low confidence)
- **Cash flow:** unknown
- **Compute today:** Sold through AWS Marketplace (AWS relationship, not a disclosed compute mega-contract): https://www.alpha-sense.com/press/alphasense-ai-search-and-market-intelligence-platform-now-available-in-new-aws-ai-marketplace-agents-and-tools-category . It uses OpenAI, Anthropic and Google frontier models plus open-weight models in its harness (5 Aug post). No GPU or neocloud deal disclosed.
- **For and against:** For: founder-led (Jack Kokko), raised $350M in the last 12 months, $600-700M+ ARR, explosive token growth, and it is training its own model. Against: GPU need is inferred, not stated. Large enough to default to AWS. Own-model serving is only part of the workload.
