# Daily GPU offtaker run, 26 Nov 2026

**Result:** 3 new companies qualify. The list was not padded to 10.

## Why only 3

Three angles were searched:
- new rounds: about 60 days of TechCrunch, Crunchbase News, TechFundingNews, SiliconANGLE, GeekWire, BetaKit, The AI Insider and TechStartups, plus about 490 AI rounds in Presspool's weekly lists;
- inference demand: OpenRouter's provider list and the sites of model-serving companies;
- physical AI, science and regulated buyers: hiring boards plus the EU tender database (TED) and UK Contracts Finder.

Every $30M+ round from the last 60 days by a company that trains or serves its own models is already in the dedup record or the reject list. Most of what remains is:
- app-layer software on third-party models;
- hardware or chip companies;
- neoclouds;
- China-based companies.
