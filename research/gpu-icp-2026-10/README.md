# Raion GPU capacity ICP: October 2026

Who needs GPU capacity 120–180 days from 2 Oct 2026 (that is, live between 30 Jan and 1 Apr 2027) in the US, UK and Europe. Contacts are C-level decision makers only.

Live page: https://claude.ai/artifact/WTP7cT2aTD1CaUQgSMp6SG (private until shared from the page's Share menu).

## The ICP in one paragraph

Raion's best buyers for this window are funded AI companies that train or serve their own models and need dedicated capacity of about 200–4,000 GPUs. That is one to twenty 0.5 MW blocks on a 12–36 month term. In practice that means $50M+ raised and a latest round in the last six months. Behind them come regulated and sovereign buyers in the UK and EU (quant firms, pharma, banks, telcos, public sector) who want capacity they control in their own jurisdiction. Capital and power owners are a third group that fits the SPV model. Dedicated capacity elsewhere is booked 6+ months ahead, so everyone who needs GPUs in February–March 2027 is choosing a supplier now.

## Method and confidence

- Accounts and signals come from public web sources on 2 Oct 2026. Every signal carries a source URL.
- Contacts were checked against press coverage, LinkedIn search results and Explorium profile data.
  - **high:** the current title is confirmed (76 contacts).
  - **medium:** the name is confirmed, but the title comes from earlier reporting or a single data source (31 contacts).
  - **low:** unconfirmed (10 contacts).
- LinkedIn URLs come only from search results or Explorium; none were guessed.
- Market figures from aggregator sites are directional. Check them before using them in external material.
