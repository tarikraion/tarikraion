What to say
  • Lead with the timing: "Sign in Q4 2026, run in Q1 2027." Raion's capacity is live from November; elsewhere the wait is 6–12 months.
  • Lead with B300 and GB300. Offer Vera Rubin in later blocks to head off "we'll wait for Rubin".
  • Quote against public price indices (SemiAnalysis, Ornn, and the new ICE and CME GPU futures). CFOs can check them.
What to prepare
  • Expect "are you rated?" Have burn-in reports, SLAs and a test window ready, and apply for a ClusterMAX rating.
  • Whether buyers will pay more for ownership and sovereignty is still untested. Ask about it in every discovery call.
  • Send seed and Series A teams to a pooled or forum offer. A full block is about $7M or more a year.
